[How to Test]
1. Launch the app, sign in with the review account above, and grant Full Calendar access.
2. On the chat screen, type: "每周三下午4点在门诊三楼开课题会，提前15分钟提醒" (weekly project meeting, Wed 4 pm, remind 15 min early).
3. Orbit uses the preloaded cloud points to create an event card. After review, it writes the event into the system Calendar. Tap the card to edit time, recurrence, destination calendar and reminders; swipe left to delete.
4. You can also send a screenshot containing a schedule to test image recognition, or long-press the microphone button to test live voice transcription.
